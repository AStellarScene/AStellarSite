Fly Theft Auto 3D
Cabinet idle · the fly is waiting

What it is

You are a fly in an endless streamed world: city, parks, desert, lakes, a beach with a pier. Jack moving traffic, scatter crowds, dive-bomb cars from the air, jam the cops' radios, and lose the heat somewhere useful. Roadblocks, spike strips, and searchlights push back. It runs entirely in the browser; nothing to install. Progression lasts for the session: close the tab and the city forgets you.

Controls

  • WASD or arrow keys — drive, and steer the fly
  • E or Space — steal the car you're hovering over; Space again to hop out
  • F — horn in a car; as the fly, dive-bomb a car or jam a cop's radio
  • Shift — boost (as the fly, rise)
  • Ctrl — drift (as the fly, descend)
  • Q — radio: Crimewave, Night Drive, Panic FM
  • R — restart
  • Touch — the game brings its own stick and buttons

How this was made: I directed the design and judged the fun; AI agents wrote the code under that direction. The first 3D version was terrible. This is the one that survived.
