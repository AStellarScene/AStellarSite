About

I'm Adam Laloge, 22, living in West Harrison, Indiana. I work as an engineering test technician at a medical-device lab in Batesville, where most of my time goes to building test software: the programs that run the tests, the interfaces the technicians use, and the AI agent setups that help write them.

I'm also finishing a B.S. in UX Design at WGU, on an accelerated schedule I'm holding myself to. The degree is why several projects on this site exist: each course gets a public, working version instead of a paper nobody reads.

Outside of work and school: photography, mostly sunsets and shoreline; strength training and cardio; and side projects, including a Minecraft launcher written in Rust that lives on my GitHub.

One habit the lab burned in: I don't trust a claim I can't measure, including my own. That's why everything on this site is labeled with where it actually stands.

The software here is built with AI agents under my direction. I specify, review, and verify; the agents type.
