About

I'm Adam Laloge, 22. I work as an engineering test technician at a medical-device lab in Batesville, Indiana. Most of my time goes to building test software: the programs that run the tests, the interfaces technicians use, and the AI agent setups that help write them.

I'm also finishing a B.S. in UX Design at WGU, on an accelerated schedule I'm holding myself to. Several projects here started as coursework. I would rather ship the working version than leave it sitting in a course document.

Outside work and school, I take photographs, mostly sunsets and shoreline. I also do strength training and cardio, and I build side projects. One of them is a Minecraft launcher written in Rust, on GitHub.

One habit the lab burned in: I don't trust a claim I can't measure, including my own. That's why everything on this site is labeled with where it actually stands.

The software here is built with AI agents under my direction. I specify, review, and verify; the agents type.
