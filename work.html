A pause before small purchases. Enter a price and how often it recurs, and see the cost as days of delay on a savings target. There is a 48-hour hold list for undecided items. Local data only, no bank connection.

Atlas

Now: Complete

The site drawn as a galaxy instead of listed as pages. Every project, photo, book, world, and post is a point of light set near the things it belongs with, and points connect when you probe them. A list shows pages. A map shows how the work connects. Nothing is placed by hand; the map generates itself from the site's content and redraws as the site grows.
