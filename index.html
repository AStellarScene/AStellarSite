Status: Prototype and usability test in progress

A testing simulator that runs in the browser. Compose a test in a visual builder, run it against a simulated device, and receive a traceability report. A planner for self-paced degree programs. Enter your courses and a target finish date and get a week-by-week plan with an honest ahead-or-behind readout. Generalized from the system I use to run my own degree.

Slip

Status: Planned

A pause before small purchases. Enter a price and how often it recurs, and see the cost as days of delay on a savings target, with a 48-hour hold list for items you are deciding on. Local data only, no bank connection.

About

How I work

I test medical devices for a living. That work teaches one habit fast: a claim without a measurement behind it is a hope. I build software the same way. Every system I ship gets tested against the people who will actually use it, and the results decide what changes next.

I work full stack: the interface, the backend, the AI agent workspaces, and the embedded hardware the software drives. The research comes first, because the interface is only ever as good as my understanding of the operator standing in front of it.

The software on this site is built with AI agents under my direction. I specify, review, and verify; the agents type.

Off the clock, I photograph sunsets and landscapes. The sky on this page is not a stock choice.
