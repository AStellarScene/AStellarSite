Reading

The reading shelf

I read adult fantasy, the dark end of it: magic, dragons, and worlds that don't pull punches. Not YA, and not romance wearing a fantasy costume.

The bar

Christopher Buehlman

Between Two Fires

The standard. Plague-era France, a disgraced knight, and a war between heaven and hell fought at ground level. Everything else on this shelf gets measured against it.

Up next, undecided

Six books I'm picking between. I haven't started one yet; the shelf moves when I do.

John Gwynne

The Shadow of the Gods

Grimdark with dragons, and the one that asks the least homework up front. Current front-runner.

Evan Winter

The Rage of Dragons

The fast one. A dragon war and a nobody fighting his way up through it.

Jenn Lyons

The Ruin of Kings

First of A Chorus of Dragons. Prophecy, a stolen identity, and dragons at the center of it.

Ryan Cahill

Of Blood and Fire

Classic-shape epic with dragon riders, written for adults.

Steven Erikson

Gardens of the Moon

Malazan, book one. The deep end: a huge world that explains nothing and trusts you to keep up.

R. Scott Bakker

The Darkness That Comes Before

The darkest of the six. Fair warning I gave myself: the dragons here are ancient history, mostly backstory.
